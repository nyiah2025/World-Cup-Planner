Short Answer

UK fans have it better than almost any other country in the world for World Cup 2026 coverage. Every single one of the 104 matches is free to watch — no subscription, no paywall, no pay-per-view. BBC and ITV split coverage of the full tournament between them, and both broadcast free over the air and online via BBC iPlayer and ITVX.

The bottom line: If you're in the UK and have a working TV, a phone, or a laptop — you can watch every World Cup 2026 match for free. Full stop.

BBC vs ITV — Who Shows What

BBC Sport and ITV Sport share exclusive UK broadcasting rights for the 2026 World Cup. ITV will kick off the tournament with the first two matches, including Mexico vs South Africa live from Mexico City at 8pm UK time on June 11, broadcast on ITV1, ITV4, ITVX, STV and STV Player.

ITV will air 29 group-stage matches, with both broadcasters sharing coverage of the final. The BBC holds the bulk of the England knockout fixtures should the Three Lions progress.

The BBC will hold three of the top four picks in the Round of 16 and three of the top five selections in the Round of 32. England's R32, R16 and semi-final matches will be on the BBC if they progress.

England vs Croatia on June 17 is the first standout fixture for UK fans — a rematch of the 2022 and 2018 World Cup clashes between two sides with genuine tournament pedigree.

West Coast evening matches push into the early hours of the morning for UK viewers. Worth checking the exact times before committing to a late night.

Watching UK Streams Abroad

BBC iPlayer and ITVX are geo-restricted to the UK. If you're travelling outside the UK during the tournament — on holiday, for work, or even in Ireland — you won't be able to access either service without a VPN.

A VPN (Virtual Private Network) lets you connect to a UK server and appear to be browsing from the UK, allowing you to access BBC iPlayer and ITVX from anywhere in the world. Top-rated options for streaming World Cup matches include NordVPN, ExpressVPN, and Surfshark — all available from the How to Watch page.

Note: using a VPN to access geo-restricted services may conflict with the broadcaster's terms of service. Always verify local laws and regulations.
