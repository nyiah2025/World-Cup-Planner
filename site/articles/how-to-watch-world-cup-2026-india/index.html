The Situation for Indian Fans

On June 1, 2026 — just ten days before the tournament opener — Zee Entertainment confirmed they have secured the official broadcast rights for the 2026 FIFA World Cup in India. The deal ends the most notable rights gap of any major market in the tournament, with India having been without a confirmed broadcaster right up until the eve of the competition.

This mirrors the pattern of the 2022 Qatar World Cup, where Viacom18 stepped in at a similar late stage to broadcast on Sports18 and JioCinema. For 2026, it is Zee Entertainment's turn — with coverage expected via ZEE5 (their streaming platform) and ZEE TV (linear broadcast). The deal covers India, where rights had previously remained unsold.

Updated June 1, 2026: Zee Entertainment is now India's official licensed broadcaster. Indian fans can watch the 2026 FIFA World Cup on ZEE5 and ZEE TV. For fans who prefer a free option, VPN access to BBC iPlayer or SBS On Demand remains available.

Setting up an account from India

For BBC iPlayer, you need to create a BBC account and enter a UK postcode during registration. Any valid UK postcode works — use a publicly listed one such as SW1A 1AA (Buckingham Palace) or W1A 1AA (BBC Broadcasting House) if you don't have a personal UK postcode.

For ITVX, registration is simpler — just an email address and password. A UK postcode is not required during sign-up, though you will need your VPN active to access content.

For SBS On Demand, registration requires only an email address and password, with no Australian address verification. Create your account on the SBS website before the tournament and keep your VPN active when watching.

Recommended approach: Set up SBS On Demand as your primary stream for all 104 matches. Add BBC iPlayer as a backup — both require an active VPN connection to an Australian or UK server respectively.

Step-by-step: watching via VPN from India

The process takes under ten minutes the first time:

1. Subscribe to a VPN (NordVPN or ExpressVPN recommended) and install the app on your phone, tablet, or computer.
2. Connect to an Australian server (for SBS) or a UK server (for BBC iPlayer or ITVX).
3. Open your browser or the streaming app. Create your free account if you haven't already.
4. Start watching. Keep the VPN connected throughout — disconnecting mid-stream will interrupt the broadcast.

Speed tip: Choose a VPN server geographically close to you where possible — for India, Singapore-based Australian servers (offered by most major VPNs) tend to give better speeds than direct Australian connections. For UK streams, servers in Singapore, Dubai, or Hong Kong often perform well as relay points.

Kickoff Times in IST

India Standard Time (IST) is UTC+5:30, which puts most World Cup matches firmly in the late-night and early-morning bracket. The 2026 World Cup is hosted across the United States, Canada, and Mexico — all operating in North American time zones, which are between 10.5 and 14.5 hours behind IST.

This is the one genuine drawback for Indian fans. Unlike the 2022 Qatar World Cup, which gave Indian fans afternoon and evening kickoffs due to the Middle East time zone, the North American schedule means most group stage matches kick off between 10:30pm and 7:30am IST.

The bright side: The 12:00pm ET kickoffs — which land at 10:30pm IST — are manageable for night-owl viewers. And any match starting at 6:00pm PT on the West Coast gives you a 7:30am IST kickoff, making it the most civilised slot for Indian fans across the entire 104-match schedule.

Zee Entertainment: India's Official Broadcaster

On June 1, 2026, Zee Entertainment announced they had secured the official broadcast rights for the 2026 FIFA World Cup in India — a last-minute deal that arrives just ten days before the tournament opener on June 11. The announcement ends months of uncertainty for Indian fans and closes the most notable rights gap in the global tournament.

The deal is consistent with India's pattern at recent World Cups — the 2022 Qatar tournament also saw rights land with Viacom18 (Sports18 / JioCinema) in the weeks just before kick-off. For 2026, Zee Entertainment step in with their ZEE5 streaming platform and ZEE TV linear channel. Exact details on pricing tiers, whether a free streaming option will be offered, and which matches will be on which platform are expected to be confirmed by Zee in the coming days.

Already confirmed: Zee Entertainment hold the official rights. Watch on ZEE5 (streaming app) or ZEE TV (linear broadcast). Check ZEE5's website for the latest subscription and match schedule details as the tournament approaches.

Will India ever qualify for the World Cup?

India did not qualify for the 2026 FIFA World Cup. The Blue Tigers are ranked 126th in the world (FIFA, May 2026) and compete in the AFC (Asian Football Confederation) zone, where qualification is fiercely competitive. India have never appeared at a FIFA World Cup in the modern era — their only appearance was in 1950, when they withdrew before playing a match.

Despite this, India is one of the world's largest football-watching markets. The sport has a passionate following, particularly in West Bengal, Goa, Kerala, and the northeast — and support for clubs like Real Madrid, Barcelona, Manchester United, and Liverpool is enormous. For many Indian fans, the World Cup is followed through the lens of a favourite European or South American nation rather than their own team.
