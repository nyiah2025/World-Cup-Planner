Short Answer

As the primary host nation of the 2026 World Cup, the USA has the most comprehensive tournament broadcast setup ever assembled. All 104 matches are available across two broadcaster partnerships — FOX Sports in English and Telemundo in Spanish — with every single game also available to stream online.

How to Watch Free Without Cable

There are several genuinely free or near-free ways to watch World Cup 2026 in the USA, depending on your language preference and setup.

Option 1 — Digital antenna (completely free)

All 70 FOX network matches — including every knockout round match from the Round of 32 to the Final — are broadcast free over the air. A one-time antenna purchase (typically $20–$50) gives you permanent free access to all FOX programming including the World Cup. This is the best value option if you don't already have cable or a streaming service.

Option 2 — Telemundo over the air (Spanish, free)

Telemundo broadcasts 92 of the 104 matches free over the air in Spanish. If you watch in Spanish, a digital antenna covers almost the entire tournament for free.

Option 3 — Tubi (free with ads)

Tubi will launch a dedicated FIFA World Cup FOX Hub featuring FOX Sports programming and tournament content. Live matches and related programming can be streamed through the Tubi app and website without a paid subscription — no MVPD authentication required.

Option 4 — Peacock via Walmart+ (Spanish)

Walmart+ subscribers get Peacock Premium included in their membership, giving access to all 104 matches in Spanish on Peacock at no additional cost.

Free trial options: FOX One offers a 7-day free trial. YouTube TV offers a 21-day free trial. FuboTV and DirecTV offer 5-day free trials. These cover enough of the group stage to watch your team's first matches without paying anything.

USMNT Match Schedule

The United States Men's National Team are in Group D alongside Australia, Paraguay, and Türkiye. As the primary host nation, all USMNT group matches are expected to air on the main FOX network — primetime, maximum audience.

Host nation advantage: As a co-host playing home matches across the country, USMNT games are expected to set record broadcast audiences. The USA vs Paraguay opener in Los Angeles is projected to be one of the most-watched soccer broadcasts in American TV history.

USMNT knockout schedule

If the USA advances from Group D, their Round of 32 match would fall between July 1–8 and their Round of 16 between July 10–13. All USMNT knockout matches air on FOX. Specific dates and venues depend on the group stage result and bracket position.
