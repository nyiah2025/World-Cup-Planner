What Time Does the World Cup Start in My Country? The Fan's Sleep Guide

Every four years, football fans around the world make the same silent calculation: how much sleep am I actually willing to sacrifice for this? The 2026 World Cup runs across three countries and four North American timezones, which means depending on where you live, your viewing schedule ranges from "perfectly convenient" to "I need to have a serious conversation with myself about my priorities."

I built the timezone tool on this site because I was tired of doing that calculation manually. But knowing what time a game kicks off in your timezone is only half the battle. The other half is deciding whether your alarm is worth setting. This guide does both — region by region, honest verdict included.

Check your team's exact kickoff times right here.

UK and Ireland — The Comfortable Ones

Let's start with the fans who drew the reasonable straw. With most North American kickoffs falling between 6pm and midnight ET, UK and Ireland viewers are looking at games running from roughly 11pm to 5am GMT during the group stage — but the bulk of the action lands in the early-to-mid evening window.

The standard kickoff slots translate as follows for UK fans:

ET Kickoff UK / Ireland (GMT) Verdict
12pm ET5pm GMTPerfect. You're home from work, the pub is open, life is good.
3pm ET8pm GMTPrime time. This is the sweet spot.
6pm ET11pm GMTManageable. Late but not unreasonable for a big game.
9pm ET2am GMTHere's where it gets interesting.

The 2am games are the ones that separate the committed fans from the casual ones. Group stage games featuring South American and African nations tend to land in the later slots. If your team plays at 2am UK time, you already know you're staying up. The only question is whether you admit it to yourself now or pretend you'll watch the highlights.

Verdict
One of the better timezones for this tournament. The late games exist but they're manageable. Set the alarm for the 2am ones — you'll regret the highlights.

Western Europe — One Hour Behind Comfort

Central European Time runs one hour ahead of the UK, which sounds like an advantage until you realise it means those 2am UK games are 3am for you. The evening games are better — 9pm ET becomes midnight CET, which is late but liveable for a big match.

The final on July 19th at MetLife Stadium kicks off at approximately 11pm ET — which is 5am CET. That's a sunrise final for Western Europe. Whether that's a problem or a feature depends entirely on how deep your team goes.

Verdict
Solid for the group stage. The knockout rounds get progressively more antisocial the later into the tournament you go. Plan your July accordingly.

Caribbean and Central America — Midnight Is Just Movie Time

This is my region, so I'll be direct: the timezone situation here is actually fine. Based in St. Vincent and the Grenadines, my latest matches kick off around midnight. That's it. Midnight.

I know that sounds late. But here's the thing — midnight is normal movie viewing time for me anyway. The house is quiet, everybody's asleep, I have the TV entirely to myself. No commentary from the couch. No negotiating over what to watch next. Just me, the match, and whatever Caribbean snack situation I've assembled.

The early games are the only slight inconvenience — a 12pm ET kickoff lands at noon local time, which is fine if you're working from home and less fine if you're not. But the prime time games are genuinely prime time here.

As for sleep — I used to complain about whoever has my voodoo doll refusing to let me sleep. All I ask is that they wait until August. The World Cup final is July 19th. After that, you can do whatever you want with it.

Verdict
Genuinely one of the better regions for this tournament. Midnight kickoffs are the ceiling, not the floor. Check your team's times and you'll probably be pleasantly surprised.

East Coast USA and Canada — The Lucky Ones

If you're watching from New York, Toronto, Miami or Boston, congratulations — you drew the best timezone in the entire tournament. The 2026 World Cup was built for you.

ET Kickoff What That Means
12pm ETLunchtime football. Set up your laptop, pretend you're working.
3pm ETWeekend afternoon perfection. Weekday afternoon requires creative scheduling.
6pm ETEarly evening. Dinner and a match.
9pm ETPrime time. This is when the big games happen.

The only games that push past midnight ET are the rare late Pacific coast kickoffs, and even those finish before 1am. East Coast fans have essentially no sleep sacrifice to make for this entire tournament. The rest of the world would like a word.

Verdict
Ideal. You have no complaints and you should feel slightly guilty about it.

West Coast USA and Canada — Morning Problems, Evening Paradise

Los Angeles, Seattle, San Francisco and Vancouver sit three hours behind the East Coast — which cuts both ways.

The good news: evening games are perfect. A 9pm ET kickoff is 6pm PT — early evening, still daylight, ideal viewing conditions. The bad news: a 12pm ET kickoff is 9am PT. That's not terrible for a weekend. For a Tuesday in the office, that's a problem you need to solve creatively.

The West Coast also hosts some of the tournament's biggest matches — Los Angeles is one of the most significant venues in the draw. USA vs Türkiye, Spain fixtures, knockout games — a lot of the biggest nights happen in your timezone's sweet spot.

Verdict
Evenings are perfect, mornings are manageable. The tournament was partially built in your backyard. Enjoy it.

West Africa — The Underrated Timezone

Ghana, Senegal, Nigeria, Côte d'Ivoire — West African fans are watching this tournament on GMT or GMT+1, which puts them in almost exactly the same position as the UK. Evening ET games land around 11pm–2am local time, which is late but not brutal.

The group stage features several West African nations — Ghana in Group L, Senegal in Group I, Côte d'Ivoire in Group E — and their games are spread across various kickoff slots. Some will be perfectly timed, some will push past midnight. Given the quality of teams involved, the midnight games are worth every minute.

Verdict
Solid timezone for this tournament. Comparable to UK viewing conditions. The late games are the price of following competitive African nations — worth paying.

East Africa and the Middle East — The Late Night Shift

Nairobi, Cairo, Riyadh, Dubai — East Africa and the Middle East run GMT+2 to GMT+4, which means the late ET games land between 2am and 4am local time. The evening games are manageable — a 6pm ET kickoff is 1am in Nairobi, midnight in Cairo. The 9pm ET games push past 3am.

Morocco are one of the tournament's most anticipated teams and their matches will draw enormous audiences across North Africa and the Middle East. Most of those games will kick off between midnight and 4am local time depending on which slot FIFA assigned them.

Verdict
The late games require commitment. For neutral viewing it's a tough ask. For your own team — set the alarm, make the coffee, accept what you are.

South Asia — The Mixed Bag

India, Pakistan, Bangladesh and Sri Lanka sit at GMT+5:30 to GMT+6, which creates an interesting split. Early ET games — noon kickoffs — land at 9:30pm to 10pm local time, which is actually perfect evening viewing. The problem is the later games. A 9pm ET kickoff lands at 6:30am the next morning in India. That's not a late night — that's an early morning with no sleep.

South Asian football fanbases are among the most passionate in the world — Brazil and Argentina both have enormous followings across the subcontinent. The commitment those fans show to watching their teams at 6am speaks for itself.

Verdict
Early games are perfect, late games are brutal. Plan your sleep schedule around your team's specific kickoff slots. The timezone tool exists precisely for this situation.

Australia and New Zealand — The Martyrs

Every World Cup, Australian and New Zealand fans do what they always do — set alarms for 4am, watch football in the dark, go to work on two hours sleep, and refuse to apologise for any of it. 2026 is no different.

A standard 3pm ET kickoff lands at 5am AEST in Sydney. A 9pm ET kickoff is 11am the next day — actually fine for a weekend. The brutal slot is the noon ET games, which arrive at 2am Sydney time. Not late. Early.

New Zealand is even further behind — add two hours to Australia's pain. A noon ET match kicks off at 4am NZST in Auckland. This is why New Zealand fans are among the most dedicated football supporters on the planet. They don't have a choice.

The good news for both nations: New Zealand are actually at this tournament. The All Whites making their first World Cup appearance since 2010 means Kiwi fans have a genuine reason to set those 4am alarms beyond pure football love.

Verdict
You already know you're setting the alarm. You've been doing this for decades. The only question is whether you tell your employer in advance or just show up looking like that.

The Honest Bottom Line

No matter where you are in the world, at least some of the 2026 World Cup is going to cost you sleep. That's the deal. It's always been the deal. The timezone tool on this site won't fix that — but it will at least tell you exactly which nights to brace for and which ones you can watch in peace.

Check your team's full schedule, find out when your alarm needs to go off, and make your peace with it now. The tournament starts June 11th. I'll be up at midnight with the TV to myself.
