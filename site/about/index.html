About MyTeamKickOff.com

Why I started myteamkickoff.com and what you can expect on this site.

01 — Why I Built This

Why I Built This

I'm from St. Vincent and the Grenadines in the Caribbean, and if you know anything about following football from this part of the world, the timezone thing is a constant headache. Every major tournament, you're doing mental math, trusting someone's WhatsApp message, and occasionally finding out you missed kick-off because someone converted the time wrong.

When the 2026 World Cup draw happened, I went looking for a clean tool where I could just pick my team and see their games in my local time. What I found were schedule pages locked to US Eastern time, or bloated sports sites where you had to dig through everything just to find your team's fixtures.

So I built my own.

02 — What's on the Site

What's on the Site

The main tool is simple — pick any of the 48 qualified nations and see every one of their fixtures converted to your local timezone automatically. No sign-up, no app, no math.

Beyond that, I've put together:

  • How to Watch guides for around 30 countries, so you know which broadcaster or streaming service to use once you know when your team plays
  • A downloadable wallchart you can print or save for the full tournament
  • A sweepstake kit if you're running one with friends, family, or your workplace
  • An articles section with country previews, viewing guides, and other bits I think fans will find useful

Everything is free to use.

03 — How the Site Is Funded

How the Site Is Funded

Like most free websites, myteamkickoff.com is supported by display advertising and some affiliate links — mainly VPN and streaming services on the Watch pages. If you click an affiliate link and make a purchase, I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

I'd rather be upfront about that than pretend the site runs on goodwill alone.

Affiliate links: Some links on the How to Watch pages — particularly VPN services and streaming platforms — are affiliate links marked with an asterisk (*). Editorial independence: Advertising and affiliate relationships have no influence over which broadcasters, platforms, or tools are featured or how they are described. The information on this site reflects honest research, not paid placement.
