01 — The Problem

It shouldn't be this hard to know when your team plays

Picture the scene. The 2026 FIFA World Cup is underway. Your team has a massive group stage match and you want to watch it live. You search online. You find a schedule — but it's in Eastern Time. You do the math. Or you think you do. You set an alarm. You wake up at 3am. The match kicked off two hours ago.

It's a small frustration, but it happens to millions of football fans every major tournament. The World Cup is a truly global event — 48 teams, 104 matches, fans in every timezone on earth — and yet finding a clear, simple answer to "what time is my team playing?" is still harder than it should be.

"What time is my team playing?" is one of the most searched questions during any World Cup. We built the clearest possible answer to it.

That frustration is exactly what myteamkickoff.com was built to solve. No schedules in a single timezone. No conversion math. No digging through a wall of fixtures to find your team. Just: pick your team, see your kickoff times. Done.

02 — What This Site Does

One tool. Every match. Your timezone.

myteamkickoff.com is a free, no-signup timezone converter built specifically for the 2026 FIFA World Cup — the largest in the tournament's history, hosted across the United States, Canada, and Mexico from June 11 to July 19.

The site covers all 104 matches across all 12 groups and every knockout round, from the group stage opener to the Final at MetLife Stadium in New York. Pick any of the 48 qualified nations from the team picker and you'll instantly see every one of their fixtures converted to your local timezone — with the venue, date, and a one-tap option to copy or share the times with friends.

Alongside the match times tool, the How to Watch guide covers official broadcasters and streaming platforms for 29 countries — so once you know when your team plays, you know exactly where to watch them too.

Everything runs in your browser. There's no account, no app to download, no subscription. The site auto-detects your timezone and updates every minute. It's as simple as we could make it.

05 — Transparency

How this site is funded

myteamkickoff.com is completely free to use. Like most free websites, it's supported by advertising and affiliate partnerships — and I believe you deserve to know exactly how that works.

💬 An honest note on advertising

Display advertising: This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads relevant to your browsing history. You can opt out of personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Affiliate links: Some links on the How to Watch pages — particularly VPN services and streaming platforms — are affiliate links marked with an asterisk (*). If you click one and make a purchase, I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. I only link to services I would genuinely recommend to a friend.

Editorial independence: Advertising and affiliate relationships have no influence over which broadcasters, platforms, or tools are featured or how they are described. The information on this site reflects honest research, not paid placement.

I'd rather be upfront about this than hide it in small print. The site costs money to run and time to maintain. Ads and affiliate commissions make it possible to keep everything free for you. That's the deal — and I think it's a fair one.
