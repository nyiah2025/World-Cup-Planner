How to Use Your Wallchart

Step 01
Print this page
Use File → Print or press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on Mac). The page will print in wallchart format — nav, header, and footer are automatically hidden.
Step 02
Fill in group stage scores
As each match is played, write in the scores and circle or underline the winning team. Mark advancing teams at the bottom of each group card.
Step 03
Track the knockout bracket
Fill in team names and scores as teams advance through the Round of 32, Round of 16, Quarterfinals, Semis, and Final.
💡 Tip — print in landscape orientation for best results. Set margins to minimum for more space.
