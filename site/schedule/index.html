Why I Built a Day-by-Day World Cup Match Tracker

The homepage is designed around following your team — pick a nation, see their fixtures, know when to watch. But real football fans don't just follow one team. They want to consume the whole tournament. With 104 matches across the expanded 48-team format, the old three-matches-a-day rhythm is gone. This tournament is relentless — fixtures cascade from morning to midnight across three countries and four timezones. This page strips away the group-by-group structure and lays everything out in a clean chronological timeline, day by day, as the tournament unfolds.

The Group Stage Is a Marathon

At peak group stage, matches roll across North America from early afternoon to late at night without stopping. A team kicking off in Miami is immediately followed by an evening clash in Monterrey, then a late-night fixture in Seattle. Trying to plan your viewing day by browsing group tables makes that incredibly difficult. This page solves that — one linear timeline, every kickoff in order, no bracket clutter.

Never Miss a Live Kickoff in Your Local Time

Whether you're building a personal viewing calendar or planning a watch party, every fixture on this page is automatically converted to your local timezone. Map out your entire week of football, track the final-day group qualifiers running simultaneously, and stay ahead of the cross-continental scheduling chaos. One page. Every match. Your time.
