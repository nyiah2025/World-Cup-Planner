How to Use This Kit
1 Click Print this Kit above. Cards and record sheet print together.
2 Cut out the 48 team cards along the dashed borders. Fold each one in half.
3 Drop all 48 cards into a hat, bowl, or mug. Mix them up thoroughly.
4 Each participant draws blind. Record their team on the sheet below the cards.
5 Leftover teams? Redistribute them, auction them, or put them back in for a second draw.
