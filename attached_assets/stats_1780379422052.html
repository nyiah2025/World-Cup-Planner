The Format Numbers

The 2026 World Cup is the first to use the 48-team format. Every number below follows directly from that expansion — more groups, more matches, more rounds, more days.

48
Teams
Up from 32 at every World Cup since 1998. A 50% increase in the field.
104
Total Matches
Up from 64. The 40 extra matches are split across group stage and the new Round of 32.
12
Groups
Groups A through L, four teams each. Every team plays three group matches.
72
Group Stage Matches
Up from 48. Six matches per group across 12 groups.
32
Knockout Stage Matches
Round of 32 (16) + Round of 16 (8) + QFs (4) + SFs (2) + 3rd place (1) + Final (1).
39
Days
June 11 to July 19. The longest World Cup in history.
8
Max Matches to Win
A team that wins the 2026 World Cup plays 8 matches, up from 7 in previous editions.
3
Host Nations
USA, Mexico, Canada. The first World Cup co-hosted by three countries.

The Money Numbers

The floor: Even at the lowest goals-per-game average in World Cup history — 2.21, set at Italia '90 — 104 matches would produce approximately 230 goals, comfortably exceeding Qatar 2022's record of 172. The total goals record will fall regardless of how defensively the tournament plays out.

Qatar 2022 record: 172 goals in 64 matches (2.69 per game). In 2026, even a below-average tournament produces a new record by 50+ goals.
